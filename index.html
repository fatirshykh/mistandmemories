HERE TO HELP

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before choosing your next scent.

How do I place an order?

Select your fragrance and bottle size, add it to your bag, then open the cart. Enter your delivery details and select Place Order on WhatsApp. Your order is sent only after you confirm the message in WhatsApp.

Can I order directly from a product page?

Yes. Choose the size and quantity and select Buy on WhatsApp. You'll enter your delivery information before the prepared message opens.

What are the delivery charges and delivery time?

Delivery charges, coverage and expected delivery time are confirmed by our team on WhatsApp before payment. No delivery fee is included in the website subtotal.

What payment methods are available?

Our team will confirm currently available payment methods and instructions when you place your order. Do not send payment before receiving confirmation.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order details. Changes depend on whether your order has already been processed or dispatched.

Are the product photographs and ingredients final?

This preview currently displays sample products, sample prices and illustrative fragrance notes. Final product photography, ingredient lists and policies will be updated before the store goes live.
